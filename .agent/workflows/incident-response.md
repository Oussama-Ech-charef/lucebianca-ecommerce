# Incident Response Workflow

## Related Skills

`devops`, `quality-assurance`, and `diagnosing-bugs`, with `security`, `backend`, `database`, `api-integration`, or `code-review` as required.

1. Confirm the incident, affected systems, start time, symptoms, and current impact.
2. Protect users and data: contain the issue and avoid destructive investigation.
3. Preserve relevant logs, metrics, traces, configuration, and timeline evidence.
4. Communicate status, ownership, severity, and next update clearly.
5. Restore safe service through rollback, mitigation, failover, or controlled repair.
6. Verify recovery with health checks and user-focused smoke tests.
7. Identify the root and contributing causes after stabilization.
8. Record corrective actions, prevention work, and lessons learned.
