# Plan Workflow

## Related Skills

`requirements-analysis` and `project-management`, with `architecture`, `documentation`, and `grill-me` for broad or risky plans.

1. Restate the goal and expected outcome.
2. Define in-scope and out-of-scope behavior.
3. Write acceptance criteria and edge cases.
4. Identify affected modules, files, dependencies, and required skills.
5. Choose an implementation order and verification strategy.
6. Record assumptions, risks, rollback considerations, and open questions.
7. Label confirmed requirements, proposed decisions, assumptions, open questions, and recommendations separately.
8. Request confirmation when the plan is broad, ambiguous, irreversible, or depends on an unapproved assumption.
