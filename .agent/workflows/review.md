# Review Workflow

## Related Skills

`quality-assurance`, `architecture`, and `code-review`, with `security`, `frontend`, `backend`, `database`, `devops`, `web-design-guidelines`, or `codebase-design` as relevant.

Review correctness, architecture, security, privacy, performance, accessibility, testing, documentation, and maintainability.

For each finding, provide evidence, impact, location, severity, and a practical recommendation. Classify findings as Critical, High, Medium, or Low. Separate confirmed defects from suggestions and unknowns.
