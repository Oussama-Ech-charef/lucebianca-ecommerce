# Product Design

Follow this sequence when the task needs product or interface design:

1. Business goal and measurable outcome
2. Users, roles, and needs
3. Information architecture and user journeys
4. Content and interaction rules
5. UX zoning and wireframes
6. Design tokens and component system
7. High-fidelity screens or prototype
8. Production components and states

Use atomic thinking: define foundations, reusable components, composed sections, templates, and pages. Respect accessibility, responsiveness, content clarity, and existing visual conventions. Do not jump to visual polish before understanding structure, and do not create a mockup without representing real states.
